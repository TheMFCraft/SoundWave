What SoundWave does

Device library

Scans music folders and the system, imports files or folders. Artwork comes from your files.

Player & queue

Mini player, full-screen now playing, shuffle, repeat, queue, speed and a sleep timer.

Home & search

Featured tracks, recents, mixes, moods, and search by title, artist, album or genre.

Playlists & likes

Your playlists, liked songs, and long-press actions for queue, album and artist.

Lyrics

Optional lyrics lookup via LRCLIB when title and artist are known. The audio file stays on device.

Jam

Several devices on your Wi-Fi. One player plays, the others add tracks and share control. Files stay local.

Desktop

Windows, Linux and macOS. The computer can be the player while phones send songs over.
